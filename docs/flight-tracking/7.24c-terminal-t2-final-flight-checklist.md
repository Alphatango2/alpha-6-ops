If that occurs, keep recording untouched for up to 60 seconds to capture the actual delay, then stop the validation and return evidence. **PASS requires:** no completion during either running-engine hold; fresh enhanced two-engine shutdown evidence; automatic IN/Complete after the unchanged stable guard, demonstrably independent of complete turbine spool-down; one ordered OUT/OFF/ON/IN sequence; PIREP eligibility; one successful submission; and preserved identity, actuals, event history and exactly one PIREP after cold restart. **FAIL/HOLD:** premature completion, missing/invalid enhanced capability, manual fallback needed, waiting on mechanical zero, missing or duplicate lifecycle events, submission error, lost/changed history, resurrected assignment, duplicate PIREP, or a new visible/native fault. Preserve the failed result and do not repeat steps to erase it. A successful later action does not erase an earlier failure.
